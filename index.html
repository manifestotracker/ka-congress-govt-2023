A Note on Good Faith

This tracker was created with genuine good faith — we believe in holding elected governments accountable to the promises they made publicly, and we want this government to succeed in fulfilling them for the people of Karnataka.

We are ordinary people, not journalists. There is a real possibility that we miss news, fail to update things quickly, or are unaware of developments on the ground. AI was used to evaluate the status of each promise, so there might be some inaccuracies. That is exactly why we need your help. If you know that something has been done, started, or abandoned — please tell us.

Methodology & Standards

01
Source of promises All promises are sourced from the official Karnataka Congress Manifesto 2023. 📄 Read the official manifesto →
02
How a promise is marked "Fulfilled" A promise is marked fulfilled only when backed by a verifiable government notification (official gazette, government order, press release) or credible news reporting that confirms implementation.
03
How a promise is marked "In Progress" A promise is marked in progress when a formal tender, committee, bill, or budget allocation has been initiated — with a source link attached.
04
What "Evaded" means A promise is marked evaded when the government has taken action that makes its fulfilment structurally impossible.
05
Term tracked Oath ceremony: 20 May 2023. All promises are tracked over the full 5-year term ending May 2028.
